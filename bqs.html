Survey Workflow

From master data to
shared report — fully structured.

Every bunker quantity survey follows a consistent, traceable 8-step workflow. No steps skipped. No data lost. Every action documented.

🗃️
Step 01
Master Data
Configure vessel specifications, tank calibration tables, port data, and surveyor profiles — the foundation of every accurate, consistent survey.
📝
Step 02
Create Survey
Initiate a new survey using pre-configured templates. Vessel and port data are auto-populated. Set the survey date, port of call, and delivery parameters in minutes.
👤
Step 03
Assign Surveyor
Assign the designated marine surveyor from your certified roster. The surveyor receives an automatic email notification with full survey details and secure access credentials.
⚓
Step 04
Execute Survey
The surveyor conducts the bunker quantity measurement using structured digital forms. All readings, calculations, and observations are captured in real time — no paper, no re-entry.
📊
Step 05
Track Progress
Operations managers and ship owners monitor every survey in real time from the ongoing surveys dashboard. Status updates, completion percentages, and delay alerts — all live.
📄
Step 06
Generate Report
The platform automatically generates a professionally formatted preliminary report from all survey data. Review, annotate, and approve before final publication.
✅
Step 07
Publish Survey
Once approved, the survey is published to the centralized repository. The final report is locked, timestamped, and added to the vessel's permanent digital survey record.
📧
Step 08
Share Report
Distribute the final report via automated email to all designated recipients — ship owners, charterers, P&I clubs, operators, and any other stakeholders — with delivery confirmation.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about BQS. Can't find what you're looking for? Our maritime experts are ready to help.

How does BQS handle surveys across multiple vessels simultaneously?
BQS is designed for multi-vessel operations. The ongoing surveys dashboard shows all active surveys across your entire fleet simultaneously, with real-time status, progress indicators, and individual survey details. You can assign different surveyors to different vessels, track all surveys from one central view, and generate fleet-wide reports — regardless of fleet size.
What types of bunker surveys does BQS support?
BQS supports the full range of bunker quantity surveys including on-hire, off-hire, bunkering, on-delivery, and off-delivery surveys. Pre-configured templates for each survey type are available out of the box, with the ability to create custom templates to match your organization's specific requirements and methodologies.
How is report generation and distribution handled?
BQS automatically generates professionally formatted PDF reports from survey data. Preliminary reports are available immediately after data entry is complete. Final reports are generated on publication approval. Email distribution is fully automated — configure recipient lists for each vessel or survey type, and reports are automatically sent to all designated parties when a survey is published, with delivery confirmation.
Is BQS accessible offline or in areas with poor connectivity?
Yes. BQS is designed with the maritime environment in mind. Surveyors can access and complete surveys with limited or intermittent internet connectivity. Data entered offline is cached locally and automatically synchronized to the central platform once connectivity is restored — ensuring surveys can be conducted even in remote ports or anchorages with poor connectivity.
How does the role-based access control work?
BQS supports granular role-based permissions including Ship Owner, Ship Manager, Marine Surveyor, Fleet Operator, and Administrator roles. Each role has configurable access to specific features, surveys, and vessels. For example, a surveyor can only access surveys assigned to them, while a fleet manager has visibility across all vessels. Custom roles can be configured to match your organization's structure.
How quickly can we get BQS set up and running?
BQS can be fully configured and operational within 24 hours. Our onboarding process includes guided vessel data import, master data setup, user account creation, and role configuration. We provide hands-on onboarding support and team training. Unlike traditional enterprise software, there is no months-long implementation — you'll be conducting digital surveys on day one.
Is historical survey data migrated from existing systems?
Yes. Our team provides data migration support to import existing survey records from spreadsheets, legacy systems, or other formats into BQS. Historical surveys can be uploaded as PDF documents and linked to vessel records, maintaining your complete survey history in one place from day one.
What security standards does BQS comply with?
BQS is built on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure complying with SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and GDPR requirements. All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Regular penetration testing, vulnerability assessments, and independent security audits are conducted. The platform maintains a 99.9% uptime SLA backed by multi-region redundant architecture.
