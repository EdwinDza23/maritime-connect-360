Bunker Quantity Survey (BQS) Application

Digitalise Custody Transfer, Eliminate Bunker Shortages, and Enforce Loss Control
Why Maritime Operators Choose BQS (The Value Proposition)
Fuel costs represent up to 60% of a vessel's total operating budget
. During custody transfers, even minor discrepancies in volume, density, or temperature calculations can lead to massive financial losses or engine damage
.
The BQS Application standardises and digitalises the entire bunkering survey lifecycle—from pre-delivery safety checks to final report publication
. By auditing the supplying barge, running rigorous ASTM volume-correction calculations, and providing instant dispute management tools, BQS acts as "cheap insurance" to protect your operating budget, verify delivered fuel quantity, and identify product diversion
.
Key Challenges BQS Resolves
Reconciling Ship-to-Shore Losses: Instantly identifies cargo or fuel losses where there is an unaccountable gain or unreconciled difference between the vessel and the supplying barge
.
Protecting Machinery from Off-Spec Fuel: Captures, records, and limits laboratory test parameters for fuel density, water content, and kinematic viscosity to prevent critical engine and boiler damage
.
Preventing Supplier Product Diversion: Combats fuel theft and unauthorised fuel movement by auditing non-cargo spaces on supplying barges before and after delivery
.
Dispute Mitigation: Instantly generates legally binding Statements of Fact (SOF) and Notes of Protest (NOP) when fuel shortages, sampling issues, blending, or line-cleaning disputes occur
.
Core Capabilities: What the Product Does
1. Guided Pre-Survey & Safety Acknowledgements
Operational Sequence Control: Digitally logs the bunkering tank sequence, nominated fuel grades (supports up to 3 grades), requested manifold pressures, and pumping rates
.
Interactive Caution & Safety Checklist: Enforces safety by recording whether the Chief Engineer was formally advised against pre-signing delivery documents or making internal fuel transfers during custody transfer
.
2. Precision Vessel Measurement (VMR) & ROB Assessment
Remaining On-Board (ROB) Assessment: Captures exact arrival quantities of fuel oil, gas oil, diesel, lubes, sludge, and sediment
.
Dynamic Tank Corrections: Integrates drafts, trim, list, and sea state to automatically apply volume and weight correction factors using ASTM tables
.
Automatic Out-Turn Calculation: Measures opening and closing tank readings to calculate the precise net bunker quantity received by the vessel
.
3. Supplying Barge Auditing (BTMR & Barge-NC)
Barge Measurement Reports (BTMR): Records opening and closing barge tank soundings, temperatures, and density readings to verify the quantity dispatched from the supplier
.
Non-Cargo Tank Declarations (Barge-NC): Audits ballast tanks, void spaces, and bilges on the barge before and after delivery to ensure no fuel is diverted
.
4. Mass Flow Meter (MFM) & Conventional Bunkering Dual-Pathways
Modern MFM Tracking: Records forward/reverse mass inventory readings, flowmeter types, and seal registration numbers
.
Dual Calculation Workflows: Automatically routes calculations based on delivery type (MFM vs. Conventional Manual Sounding) to generate appropriate out-turn metrics
.
5. Rigorous Sampling Controls
Manifold Collection Logs: Records primary representative sample collection at the point of custody transfer (manifold)
.
Distribution & Seal Management: Manages seal numbers and distribution lists across ship, barge, surveyor, and laboratory subdivisions
.
6. Automated Protest & Dispute Management (NOP/SOF)
Statement of Facts (SOF): Automatically calculates discrepancies between nominated quantities, Bunker Delivery Notes (BDN), vessel received volumes, and barge supplied volumes
.
Notes of Protest (NOP) Generator: Formally drafts official protests with manual inputs and pre-configured checkboxes for
:
Sampling Disputes (refusal to witness/seal)
.
Bunker Quantity Shortages (short deliveries vs. BDN)
.
In-Line Blending of fuel
.
Cappuccino Bunkers (excessive, prolonged air blowing with recorded starting/ending times)
.
Other reasons (undue delays, slow pumping, late arrival)
.
7. Charter ROB On/Off Hire Certificates
Captures on-hire and off-hire certificates, recording ship draft, trim, heel, and certified fuel/freshwater/lube totals on delivery or re-delivery
.
