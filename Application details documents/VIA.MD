Vessel Inspection Application (VIA)
Standardise Vessel Inspections, Track Defects to Resolution, and Mitigate Operational Risks
Why Maritime Operators Choose VIA (The Value Proposition)
Evaluating shipboard machinery, structure, and safety systems against demanding regulatory frameworks (such as VIQ or SIRE) requires structured field reporting. However, logging defects, tracking them to closure, and assessing crew safety awareness on paper creates dangerous information siloes.
The Vessel Inspection Application (VIA/VIS) provides a unified ecosystem for fleet inspections and closed-loop defect tracking
. By combining structured questionnaires, active risk routing, and an interactive, real-time defect management dashboard, VIA ensures that critical shipboard deficiencies are captured, prioritized, and verified through to physical resolution
.
Key Challenges VIA Resolves
Fragmented Deficiency Management: Connects field findings to a continuous defect tracker with clear status states (Pending, In Progress, Closed)
.
Subjective Risk Classification: Guarantees that any safety failures are immediately graded by criticality (Critical, Non-Confirmative, Observation) with target completion dates
.
Evaluating the "Human Element": Goes beyond technical checklist compliance by evaluating crew sea tenure and auditing active voyage safety measures
.
Lag in Corrective Actions: Eliminates communication lags, allowing office superintendents and onboard crew to view and resolve defects with timestamped remarks and visual proof
.
Core Capabilities: What the Product Does
1. Structured Group & Subgroup Inspections
Technical Hierarchy Navigation: Guides surveyors through questionnaires neatly compartmentalised into technical inspection chapters (such as Hull & Superstructures, E/R Machinery, Cargo Related Equipment, Safety Equipment)
.
Direct Photo Integration: Allows surveyors to upload photos directly against specific questions during the technical audit
.
2. Active Remark & Defect Routing
Automated Decision Prompting: When a surveyor marks any inspection item as "No", the system automatically forces a choice between routing the finding to the Defect Path or Remark Path
.
Detailed Deficiency Capture: The Defect Path prompts the surveyor to input
:
Granular defect descriptions.
Defect photos.
Criticality levels (Critical, Non-Confirmative, Observation).
Target completion dates.
Qualitative Comment Logs: The Remark Path routes the finding as a descriptive comment for operational context
.
3. Closed-Loop Defect Lifecycle Tracking
Central Defects Dashboard: Aggregates and displays all flagged deficiencies on a master defects list
.
Continuous Status States:
Pending: Set automatically the moment a defect is flagged
.
InProgress: Updates once the superintendent or crew accesses the dashboard to add corrective actions
.
Closed: Changed manually once the deficiency is physically corrected, supported by verification remarks and closure images
.
4. Crew Composition & Safety Awareness Matrix
Granular Experience Profiles: Records the sea tenure in years and months across senior officers (Master, Chief Engineer, Chief Officer, First Engineer)
.
Tenure Audits: Logs overall sea experience, company service, time onboard, and time in their current rank
.
Safety Awareness Auditing: Captures safety meetings held onboard, topics discussed, and records formal surveyor assessments of crew safety/environmental familiarity
.
5. Attendance Milestones & Onboard Verification
Milestone Logs: Records whether onboard safety meetings were held, emergency drills were conducted, training seminars occurred, PMS records were verified, and critical machinery was tested in the presence of the attending superintendent
.
6. Visual Progress Gates & Report Compilation
Completion Status Dashboard: Displays the total question count (e.g., 606 questions) against completed questions with color-coded status blocks (In Progress, Pending, Completed) for each group
.
Preliminary & Final Reports: Supports on-the-go preliminary PDF generation and auto-compiles final report PDFs upon review, with download links sent automatically via email
.

Administrative Control & System Customisation
Administrators retain central template and system control through Master Details panels
:
Categories & Sub-Categories Builder: Standardise the inspection framework to align with proprietary checklists or standard international templates
.
Dynamic Question Pool: Create, edit, and paginate inspection questions mapped directly to specific categories and sub-categories
.
Role-Based Security: Manage user accounts, update roles (e.g., Admin, Surveyor), and enable/disable users
.
Who VIA is For
Ship Owners & Technical Superintendents: Maintain absolute control over fleet deficiencies, track PMS health, and monitor open defect lists across multiple vessels.
Attending Superintendents & Marine Surveyors: Eliminate spreadsheet-based defect tracking and generate structured audit reports in the field.
Compliance, Safety, & Quality Assurance (HSEQ) Officers: Keep a continuous audit trail of vessel condition to ensure high performance during third-party vetting inspections.
