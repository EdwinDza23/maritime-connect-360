Vessel Condition Survey (VCS) Application

Digitalise Condition Inspections, Standardise Vessel Audits, and Streamline Fleet Reporting
Why Maritime Operators Choose VCS (The Value Proposition)
Evaluating vessel seaworthiness, cargo-worthiness, and operational conditions is critical to protecting high-value marine assets and ensuring compliance. Yet, surveyors are often bogged down by non-standard checklists, manual formatting of massive photo logs, and fragmented reporting workflows.
The Vessel Condition Survey (VCS) application bridges the gap between field surveyors and head offices
. It replaces manual checklists with dynamic, multi-dimensional questionnaires, automates photo-log collation, evaluates the "human element" via granular crew experience tracking, and generates professional, publication-ready reports at the click of a button
.
Key Challenges VCS Resolves
Checklist Subjectivity: Eliminates inconsistent grading by guiding surveyors through standardized question pathways linked directly to physical spaces or technical departments
.
Unstructured Visual Evidence: Halts the chaotic handling of photo attachments by organising images directly into custom vessel segments with captions
.
Cumbersome Office Drafting: Replaces days of manual layout work with instant, one-click preliminary and final report compilation in both PDF and Word formats
.
Incomplete Survey Submissions: Prevents skipped assessments via an intelligent publish gate that scans for unanswered questions, missing crew data, and undocumented compartments
.
Core Capabilities: What the Product Does
1. Dynamic, Location-Based & Categorised Questionnaires
Two-Way Questionnaire Navigation: Surveyors can easily navigate inspection lists by physical location (such as Deck Chief Officer, Deck and Engine Room) or thematic category (Personnel Safety, Schedule Reliability, Safety of Vessel & Cargo)
.
Standardised Grading (YES / NO / N/A / N/I): Standardises responses with clear grading, including "Not Investigated" (N/I)
.
Mandatory Evidentiary Prompts: Selecting specific responses dynamically enables descriptive comment blocks, prompting surveyors to capture qualitative text, upload photos, and insert captions in the field
.
2. Custom Media & Document Segment Management
Custom Inspection Compartments: Surveyors can build dedicated compartments (e.g., Vessel Bow, Astern and Parallel Body, Access Ladders, Gangway) to segment inspection logs
.
Bulk Photo Processing: Upload multiple pictures simultaneously, preview them on a dedicated slide interface, insert hover-editable descriptions, or delete unneeded photos
.
Targeted Document Storage: Create custom compartments to archive critical onboard documents (such as Condition of Class (CoC), Flag Dispensation, Critical Spare Parts Inventory, or Engine Maintenance Schedules)
.
3. Human Element Assessment (Crew Experience Matrix)
Service History Benchmarks: Maps sea experience in exact years and months across senior officers (Master, Chief Engineer, Chief Officer, First Engineer)
.
Tenure Tracking: Benchmarks officer service across four maritime metrics: total sea service, current company tenure, onboard tenure, and duration in their current rank
.
4. Intelligent Quality & Submission Gates
Submit-for-Review Scanner: Validates entire jobs prior to submission, scanning each section for compliance
.
Actionable Error Flags: Displays a clear summary page indicating exactly how many questions remain unanswered in each category and lists missing images, files, or crew experience data
.
5. One-Click Reporting (PDF & Editable Word)
On-the-Go Preliminary PDFs: Generates a real-time snapshot of the survey information and active questionnaire responses from any screen
.
Professional Final Deliverables: Instantly compiles final reports into PDF and editable MS Word (DOCX) formats containing polished headers, crew matrices, questionnaire logs, and captioned photo appendices
.
How VCS Works (The Operational Workflow)

          ▼
 [ Field Audit ]   ➔ User completes locations, answers questions, uploads media/crew logs. [73, 74]
          ▼
 [ Validation ]    ➔ Publish tab displays completeness check. Fix warnings. [75, 76]
          ▼
 [ Review Lock ]   ➔ User submits job. Status locks to prevent field modification (Status: FOR REVIEW). [73]
          ▼
 [ Quality Edit ]  ➔ Admin reviews. Can unpublish to unlock and edit if updates are needed. [73, 81]
          ▼
 [ Final Publish ] ➔ Admin publishes. final report (PDF & DOCX) generates & emails (Status: PUBLISHED). [73, 80]
Administrative Control & System Customisation
Administrators control and standardise survey requirements from the central office
:
Category & Question Creator: Create unique inspection categories and populate a standardized pool of inspection questions
.
Location/Crew Question Mapping: Map specific question blocks to physical vessel locations so surveyors only see highly relevant checklists in the field
.
Who VCS is For
Ship Operators & Fleet Managers: Perform standardized pre-vetting audits, dry-dock prep condition surveys, and pre-purchase inspections.
Marine Surveyors & Technical Consultancies: Drastically reduce administrative overhead by automating formatting and report-writing processes.
Insurers & P&I Clubs: Capture standardized, photographic risk evidence with a complete audit trail.
