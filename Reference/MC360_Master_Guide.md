# MC360 Master Guide

## Executive Summary
**Product:** MaritimeConnect360 (MC360)

**Brand Statement:** The Operating System for Modern Maritime Operations.

**Tagline:** One Platform. Total Maritime Intelligence.

### Vision
Build the world's most trusted All in One operating system for maritime companies by unifying inspections, surveys, bunker operations and compliance into one connected platform.

### Mission
Eliminate fragmented maritime software by providing one intelligent platform that connects teams, workflows and data.

## Brand Personality
- Trustworthy
- Human
- Innovative
- Intelligent

## Core Values
- Innovation
- Reliability
- Trust
- Accuracy
- Compliance

## Value Proposition
One intelligent platform to manage inspections, surveys, bunker operations and compliance.

## Problems We Solve
- Multiple disconnected applications
- Manual inspection workflows
- Fragmented maritime software
- Poor visibility across operations
- Slow reporting and compliance

## Target Users
Primary:
- Fleet Managers

Secondary:
- Ship Owners
- Operations Directors

- Compliance Teams

## Product Modules
Current

- Vessel Condition Survey
- Vessel Inspection

## UX Principles
- Product-first storytelling
- Show, don't tell
- Visual hierarchy over long copy
- Progressive disclosure
- Enterprise trust through design
